A playable prototype for developers, educators and researchers who want to make adaptive learning understandable.

01 / EXPLORE

Make room for curiosity.

Start with a gentle island adventure, then choose ordered steps, mosaics, rhythms or exploration. Change your mind without losing your progress.

02 / LEARN

Grow toward independence.

Move from concrete pieces to pictures, symbols and contextual problems. After help, a new independent opportunity checks the next step.

03 / UNDERSTAND

Open the feedback loop.

Inspect the signal, the adaptation rule and the response that followed. Export the record and replay synthetic scenarios in the local dashboard.
